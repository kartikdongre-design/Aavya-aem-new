Our Journey Through The Years

Explore key milestones that shaped our growth from a national ceramics pioneer into a diversified manufacturing leader serving construction and industry across the region.

  • 1977

    Foundations

    Saudi Ceramics was established with a vision to deliver quality building materials for a growing Kingdom.

  • 1980s

    Early Growth

    Production capacity expanded and distribution networks reached key cities across Saudi Arabia.

  • 1990s

    Product Line Expansion

    We broadened our portfolio beyond core ceramics into sanitary ware, water heaters, and complementary building solutions—strengthening our role as a full-service partner for construction projects.

  • 2000s

    Scale & Standards

    Investments in plant modernization and quality systems supported larger projects and export readiness.

  • 2010s

    Diversification

    New categories and industrial minerals extended our footprint while keeping sustainability in focus.

  • 2020s

    Innovation Forward

    Digital tools, customer experience, and continuous improvement guide the next chapter of Saudi Ceramics.
